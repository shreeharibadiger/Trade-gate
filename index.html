News & XAUUSD

Evaluation: no news-window restriction on holding/managing positions, but purposely trading news spikes to exploit volatility is prohibited.

Master Account: only events marked Restricted in FundingPips' own Economic Calendar count. USD news affects XAUUSD. The 2-Step Pro model page states restricted news window is 5 minutes before/after; restricted speeches are 10 minutes before the speech until 10 minutes after it ends. Trades opened at least 5 hours before the event qualify for the swing-trader exception. Partial closes can flag the entire order.

Always confirm the current event label and exact window in the FundingPips dashboard. Avoid intentionally trading news even during evaluation.

Overnight & weekend

Evaluation: overnight and weekend holding allowed.

Master: overnight and weekend holding are not allowed by default unless the applicable Swing add-on is purchased. System auto-closes positions during its server-time window. Weekend auto-closed positions don't count as a valid trading day.
